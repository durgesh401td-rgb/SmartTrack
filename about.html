Our Mission

Bridging Daily Budgeting, EMI Management & Long-Term Compounding

Most budgeting apps focus only on recording expenses, while investment platforms ignore loan EMIs and day-to-day spending pressures. SmartTrack was architected to unite all three facets into one streamlined Rupee-denominated platform.

Whether you're allocating funds for monthly rent in Mumbai or Bengaluru, paying off an active home loan EMI, or building a diversified equity mutual fund portfolio, we help you track every single Rupee without requiring expensive software licenses.
